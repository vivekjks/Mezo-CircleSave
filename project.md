# CircleSave – Decentralized Group Savings Platform

**Tagline:** Save Together. Grow Together. Trust Every Transaction.

**Project Category:** DeFi / Access and Distribution

**Blockchain:** Mezo Network

**Primary Asset:** MUSD

**Project Type:** Group Savings and Community Finance Platform

## 1. Introduction – What Is CircleSave?

CircleSave is a decentralized group savings platform built on the Mezo blockchain.

It allows groups of people to save money together using MUSD through transparent, blockchain-powered savings pools.

Users can create savings groups with their friends, family members, colleagues, students, or communities.

Each group has its own savings pool, contribution schedule, membership rules, and payout mechanism.

Instead of depending on one person to collect and manage everyone's money, CircleSave uses smart contracts to record contributions and manage funds according to rules agreed upon by the group.

The platform makes group savings easier, more transparent, and more trustworthy.

### Simple Example

Imagine five friends want to save money together.

Each person agrees to contribute 100 MUSD every month.

That means:

- 5 members.
- 100 MUSD per member.
- 500 MUSD collected every month.

After five months, the group will have collected 2,500 MUSD in total contributions.

CircleSave helps organize these contributions, track payments, and distribute funds according to the group's agreed rules.

The platform can support different savings models, such as:

1. Fixed-term group savings.
2. Rotating monthly payouts.
3. Goal-based savings.
4. Community savings pools.
5. Emergency savings groups.

The main goal is to bring traditional community savings practices into a transparent decentralized application.

## 2. Problem Statement – What Problem Are We Solving?

People around the world save money together with friends, family members, colleagues, and communities.

However, traditional group savings systems often depend on manual coordination and trust.

### Problem 1: Lack of Transparency

In traditional group savings, one person may collect and manage everyone's contributions.

Other members may not know:

- Who has contributed.
- How much money has been collected.
- Who has missed a payment.
- How much money remains in the group.
- Whether the collected funds have been distributed correctly.

**Our Solution:**

CircleSave provides a transparent group dashboard where members can see contributions, total savings, payment records, and group activity.

All important financial transactions are recorded on the blockchain.

### Problem 2: Missed Contributions

Members may forget their monthly or weekly savings contributions.

This can delay the group's savings plan and affect other members.

**Our Solution:**

CircleSave provides contribution schedules, payment reminders, and notifications.

Members can easily see upcoming contribution deadlines and their payment status.

### Problem 3: Lack of Trust Between Members

Traditional savings groups often depend on one person managing the collected money.

If that person misuses funds or fails to distribute them correctly, other members can lose money.

**Our Solution:**

CircleSave uses smart contracts to manage funds according to predefined group rules.

Members can verify contributions and payouts on-chain instead of relying entirely on one person's records.

### Problem 4: Difficult Group Coordination

Organizing a savings group through messaging applications and spreadsheets can become confusing.

Members may have different payment dates, amounts, and savings goals.

**Our Solution:**

CircleSave provides a dedicated platform for creating groups, inviting members, setting contribution schedules, and tracking savings progress.

### Problem 5: No Simple Digital Savings Infrastructure for Communities

Many informal savings groups do not have access to transparent digital tools.

**Our Solution:**

CircleSave offers a simple interface where communities can organize savings pools using MUSD without requiring every member to understand blockchain technology.

## 3. Our Proposed Solution – How CircleSave Solves These Problems

CircleSave creates digital savings communities powered by MUSD and smart contracts.

The platform allows users to:

1. Create a savings group.
2. Invite friends or other members.
3. Set a savings goal.
4. Decide the contribution amount.
5. Select weekly or monthly contribution schedules.
6. Collect contributions in MUSD.
7. Track every member's payment.
8. Monitor total group savings.
9. Distribute funds according to predefined rules.
10. View the complete transaction history.

Every group operates according to its selected rules.

The smart contract records contributions and ensures that payouts follow the group's agreed conditions.

The platform does not require members to trust a single group administrator with the accounting of their money.

# 4. How CircleSave Works – Complete User Workflow

### Step 1: Connect Wallet

Users visit CircleSave and connect a supported crypto wallet.

The platform detects the connected Mezo network and displays the user's MUSD balance.

### Step 2: Create a Savings Group

A user can create a new savings group.

The creator enters:

- Group name.
- Group description.
- Maximum number of members.
- Contribution amount.
- Contribution frequency.
- Savings duration.
- Payout method.
- Group rules.

For example:

Group Name: Friends Savings Circle

Members: 5

Contribution: 100 MUSD

Frequency: Monthly

Duration: 5 Months

Total Planned Contributions: 2,500 MUSD.

### Step 3: Invite Members

After creating the group, the creator receives an invitation link.

They can share the link with friends, family members, or colleagues.

Members open the link, connect their wallets, and join the group.

The smart contract records the group membership according to its rules.

### Step 4: Members Contribute MUSD

Every member contributes the agreed amount of MUSD.

For example:

Five members contribute 100 MUSD each.

The group collects 500 MUSD.

The platform updates the group's contribution records.

Members can see who has paid and who still has pending contributions.

### Step 5: Track Group Savings

The dashboard displays:

- Total collected MUSD.
- Total target amount.
- Number of active members.
- Completed contributions.
- Pending contributions.
- Upcoming contribution deadlines.
- Savings progress.

### Step 6: Distribute Funds

When the required conditions are satisfied, CircleSave distributes funds according to the group's selected payout model.

For example, a rotating savings group may distribute the monthly collected amount to one member at a time.

A goal-based savings group may distribute the accumulated funds when its target and withdrawal conditions are met.

### Step 7: Complete the Savings Cycle

At the end of the savings period, members can view the complete savings history.

The group can close the cycle or create another savings round.

2. The creator shares it with other members.
3. Members open the invitation link.
4. Members connect their wallets.
5. Members review the savings rules.
6. Members join the group.

### Features:

- Invitation links.
- Membership limits.

**Why is this useful?**

## Feature 3: Automated Contribution Management

This feature helps members contribute MUSD according to the group's schedule.

### How it works:

1. The group establishes a contribution amount.
2. The smart contract records the contribution schedule.
3. Members receive reminders before payment deadlines.
4. Members submit their MUSD contributions.
5. The smart contract records successful payments.
6. The dashboard updates each member's payment status.

### Example:

A group has five members.

Each member contributes 100 MUSD every month.

The monthly contribution target is 500 MUSD.

CircleSave tracks each payment and shows the total collected amount.

**Why is this useful?**

It reduces manual tracking and helps members stay organized.

Important: Reminders can be automated, but recurring payments should only happen with explicit user authorization and properly designed token permissions. The first version should use member-approved contribution transactions.

## Feature 4: Rotating Savings System

This is one of the most interesting features of CircleSave.

A rotating savings system allows members to receive the group's collected contributions one at a time.

### Example:

Imagine five friends.

Each friend contributes 100 MUSD every month.

Every month, the group collects 500 MUSD.

The payout schedule is:

- Month 1: Member A receives 500 MUSD.
- Month 2: Member B receives 500 MUSD.
- Month 3: Member C receives 500 MUSD.
- Month 4: Member D receives 500 MUSD.
- Month 5: Member E receives 500 MUSD.

At the end of the cycle, every member has contributed 500 MUSD and received one 500 MUSD payout.

### How it works:

1. Members join the group.
2. Members agree on the payout order.
3. Each member contributes the required amount.
4. The smart contract tracks completed contributions.
5. Once the month's payout conditions are met, the designated member becomes eligible to receive the funds.
6. The contract distributes the payout.
7. The next payout period begins.

**Why is this useful?**

It helps groups organize community-based savings without depending on a person to manually collect and distribute money.

The contract must also define what happens if a member misses a payment or leaves the group after receiving an earlier payout.

## Feature 5: Goal-Based Savings

**Why is this useful?**

**Why is this useful?**

It provides transparency and creates a verifiable financial history for the group.

# 6. Different Savings Models Supported by CircleSave

CircleSave can support multiple savings models.

### Model 1: Rotating Savings Circle

Members contribute a fixed amount regularly.

One member receives the collected amount during each contribution period.

Every member receives one payout during the complete cycle.

**Best for:** Friends, families, and community savings groups.

### Model 2: Goal-Based Savings

Members contribute toward a shared savings target.

Funds are distributed when the target and agreed conditions are met.

**Best for:** Travel groups, events, families, and shared purchases.

### Model 3: Fixed-Term Group Savings

Members contribute regularly for a predefined period.

The accumulated funds are released when the savings period ends.

**Best for:** Long-term group savings.

### Model 4: Emergency Savings Pool

Members contribute to a shared emergency fund.

Withdrawals require the group's agreed approval process or other predefined rules.

**Best for:** Families and trusted communities.

### 1. Circle Factory Contract

Responsible for:

- Creating new savings groups.
- Deploying or registering group contracts.
- Managing group identifiers.
- Recording group configurations.

### 2. Savings Circle Contract

Responsible for:

- Managing group membership.
- Recording contributions.
- Tracking the savings period.
- Maintaining group balances.
- Enforcing group rules.

### 3. Contribution Manager

Responsible for:

- Receiving MUSD contributions.
- Recording member payments.
- Checking contribution deadlines.
- Updating contribution status.

### 4. Payout Manager

Responsible for:

- Determining payout eligibility.
- Managing payout order.
- Distributing MUSD.
- Recording completed payouts.

### 5. Membership Manager

Responsible for:

- Member registration.
- Invitation validation.
- Membership limits.
- Member permissions.

### 6. Access Control and Emergency Protection

Responsible for:

- Protecting administrative functions.
- Enforcing group rules.

# 12. Security and Risk Management

CircleSave manages funds belonging to multiple people, so security is extremely important.

Important protections include:

- Smart contract testing.
- Reentrancy protection.
- Safe token transfers.
- Prevention of unauthorized withdrawals.
- Prevention of duplicate payouts.
- Clear membership rules.
- Clearly defined contribution deadlines.
- Emergency pause functionality.
- Transparent payout calculations.
- Explicit handling of missed contributions.

The platform should also explain the risks associated with smart contracts, blockchain transactions, token approvals, and group participation.

For rotating savings groups, special attention must be given to members who receive an early payout and then fail to contribute in later periods.

The smart contract cannot magically guarantee that every member will fulfill future obligations. The group must have clear rules and safeguards for missed payments and defaults.
